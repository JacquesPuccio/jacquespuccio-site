Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 19, 2025

Overview

jacquespuccio ("we", "us", "our") operates the website jacquespuccio.com. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Ever.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide when filling out the Business Analysis Request form on this website. This may include:

  • Business name, industry, and website URL
  • Estimated annual revenue range
  • Services you are interested in
  • Description of your current business challenges and priorities
  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number or WhatsApp (optional)
  • How you found out about us
  • Any additional information you choose to share

We do not use cookies for tracking, run advertising pixels, or deploy analytics scripts on this website. Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which may process your IP address as part of serving the font files.

How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To review your business situation and prepare for an initial discovery call
  • To contact you regarding your request for analysis or services
  • To understand your needs so we can provide relevant proposals

We will not use your contact details to send unsolicited marketing emails or add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

How We Store Your Information

Form submissions are stored in a private Google Sheets document accessible only to jacquespuccio. This document is protected by Google account security and is not shared with outside parties.

We retain your information for as long as necessary to carry out a potential engagement. If you request deletion, we will remove your data promptly (see "Your Rights" below).

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services in connection with this website:

  • Google Fonts — used to serve typefaces. Google may log your IP address when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Apps Script / Google Sheets — used to receive and store form submissions. Your submitted data is stored in a private Google Workspace spreadsheet. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — used to host and serve this website. Vercel may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, page requests) for operational purposes. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

These are the only external services that may process your data in connection with your use of this website.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent for any future communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@jacquespuccio.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
